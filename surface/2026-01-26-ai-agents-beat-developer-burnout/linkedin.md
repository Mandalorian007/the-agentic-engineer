The constant pressure to ship faster is exhausting—but here's what I realized: much of the burnout comes from task overhead, not the actual work.

Scoping, researching, planning, estimating... it all adds up before you write a single line of code.

So I built a simple system: an AI agent that automatically scopes every task the moment I add it to my board.

It analyzes my repos, suggests implementation approaches, and estimates effort. When I'm ready to work, everything is pre-researched.

The mental shift has been profound. My backlog went from a source of anxiety to a menu of options I can browse based on available time.

In this post, I share the architecture and prompts that make it work.
