Content pipelines have traditionally been expensive. Character art? Hire an artist. Animations? That's a specialist. Both at scale? A team and a budget.

But AI image and video generation changes the equation. I recently built a content pipeline using Claude Code skills that turns text descriptions into polished visual assets.

The pipeline produces:
- High-quality character images from text prompts
- Discord-ready emojis (128x128, optimized)
- Animated ability clips via Sora

The key insight: individual AI capabilities become dramatically more useful when composed into workflows. Three simple scripts - image generation, emoji conversion, video animation - chain together into a complete content pipeline.

In this article, I share the architecture, the code, and a real example showing a character go from concept to complete art assets in a single session.

For prototyping, indie games, or Discord projects, the barrier to visual content just dropped dramatically.
