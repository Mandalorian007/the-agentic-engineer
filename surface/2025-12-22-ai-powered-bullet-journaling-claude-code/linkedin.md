Bullet journaling transformed my productivity - until I discovered its Achilles heel.

When you miss a day or fall off track, the system that was supposed to help becomes another source of stress. Pages of catch-up work. Mental barriers just to sit down and reorganize.

So I rebuilt the entire approach using Claude Code.

The key insight: AI can carry the cognitive load of system maintenance precisely when you're too overwhelmed to do it yourself.

My setup:
- Simple markdown files (inbox, week, quarter, projects)
- Claude Code commands for each workflow (weekly review, morning planning, recovery)
- A 'reboot' command for when I've fallen off track
- Calendar integration via sandboxed skills

The result? Missing days lost their sting. Pivoting strategies became painless. The mental barrier to planning dissolved.

The best productivity system is the one you actually use. And systems become much easier to use when they help you recover from the inevitable stumbles.
