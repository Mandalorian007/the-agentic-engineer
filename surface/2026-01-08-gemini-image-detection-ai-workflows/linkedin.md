Our AI workflows have been text-focused for too long.

We write prompts, parse responses, generate code. But when it comes to images, we treat them as static assets - upload, describe, move on.

That's a missed opportunity.

Gemini has image detection capabilities that most developers overlook entirely. You can ask it to find objects and get back precise bounding box coordinates - not just 'there's a door' but 'there's a door at position (234, 156).

This unlocks real applications:

- Accessibility tools that understand visual interfaces
- Image editing that knows what's where
- Quality control that spots specific defects
- Games that understand their own visuals

The barrier to computer vision has dropped dramatically. What used to require ML teams and custom training is now an API call with natural language.
